01 / STORY

Every technical decision starts with the problem it's meant to solve.

It's not about doing more. It's about doing what's worth doing.

I try not to look at a technical solution in isolation — I look at the problem behind it, and who actually feels it beyond QA.

Before I build anything, I look for the root cause, not just the symptom, then validate the idea the simplest way possible — the smallest version that proves it saves real time — before investing in a full solution.

I prioritize by value, not by what's easiest: how often something happens, how much time it costs, how many people it touches, and the risk if it goes wrong. An operation that takes minutes but runs hundreds of times often matters more than a complex test that runs once a month.
